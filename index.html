PDF Easy Privacy Policy

Developer: kedge
App: PDF Easy: Read, Convert & Sign
Package: com.kedge.pdfeasy
Effective date: 19 October 2026
Contact: kedgeyuan@gmail.com

This policy covers PDF Easy 1.1.0, which is free and includes InMobi banner advertising and InMobi CMP for privacy choices. It has no accounts or in-app purchases. The earlier 1.0.0 release contains no advertising or consent SDKs and does not make these advertising-related transfers.

Documents and local processing

Reading, image conversion, PDF image export, DOCX conversion, compression, merging, annotations, watermarks and handwritten signing take place on your device. PDF Easy does not transmit document contents, selected images, notes, signatures, file names or file paths to the developer or advertising services. Document processing remains available offline. Advertising and consent services use the network separately, as described below.

Imported PDFs are copied into private app storage. Document names, original file paths, favorites, tags and tag colors, page bookmarks, reading positions and preferences are stored locally. An editing draft may be kept in the app cache to restore an interrupted session. A signature is embedded into a document you create and is not saved as a reusable signature profile. The app does not offer cloud storage or cloud backup.

File selection and sharing

When you open Files, PDF Easy can request file access to discover PDF documents across shared storage. Android 11 and later use All files access; older Android versions use storage read permission. The app scans locally without uploading document contents, names or paths. It lists accessible PDFs without copying every file, and creates a private copy when you open or manage one. Other apps’ private folders remain inaccessible. You may decline or revoke this permission and continue choosing individual documents through the Android system picker. Photos also use the system picker, without broad photo-library permission. A selected cloud provider operates under its own policy.

Sharing occurs only when you choose to share a file. Android grants temporary access to the receiving app. That app and any destination you select process the shared content under their own policies. You decide whether to save copies outside the app.

PDF pages exported as PNG or JPG are saved in Pictures/PDF Easy and remain there until you delete them. Android 9 and earlier request storage write permission only when you export images to the album. Android 10 and later save these images without requesting photo-library access or All files access.

Support email

If you choose to contact support through your email app, we receive the email address, message and attachments you send. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve the reported issue. Support messages are retained only as needed for that purpose, unless retention is required by law. You may request deletion by contacting kedgeyuan@gmail.com. Your email provider handles delivery under its own policy.

InMobi advertising and consent

Version 1.1.0 directly integrates InMobi Ads and InMobi CMP. The app does not integrate AdMob, Google Mobile Ads, AppLovin MAX or a separate analytics SDK. A banner may appear on the Files screen after the applicable privacy requirements are resolved. No banner is placed in the PDF reader, editor or conversion screens. Advertising is not required to read, convert or sign documents.

InMobi and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers and app-set identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device and operating-system details, application information, advertising impressions and interactions, performance/diagnostic events and consent records. Purposes include serving and personalizing advertising where permitted, measuring advertising performance, understanding audiences, improving the advertising service, and fraud prevention. CMP requests may process IP address, region, app details and consent information to determine which privacy notice applies and remember your choices even if you decline advertising.

PDF Easy does not request precise or approximate Android location permissions, nor provide GPS coordinates, contacts or document information to the SDK. Google Play services advertising-identifier and app-set libraries may be included as InMobi dependencies; this is not a Google Mobile Ads integration.

InMobi CMP manages GDPR/TCF and applicable US privacy choices. Before privacy requirements are determined, or when the necessary consent is absent, advertising requests remain off. Declining the required consent or opting out stops further ad requests and removes the banner. You can reopen privacy choices in Settings > Advertising privacy. If the privacy service is unavailable, document tools remain usable without ads. Android also provides advertising-ID deletion/reset controls. Privacy choices and document data are separate.

InMobi and its partners process advertising data under their own retention practices and may process data internationally. Read InMobi’s privacy policy and the vendor disclosures in the CMP for recipients, purposes, retention and privacy-rights requests. You can contact us for help, or use InMobi’s privacy-rights channels. Clearing this app’s data deletes local documents and consent records; it does not by itself delete data already processed by an advertising provider.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a document from the library deletes its app-private copy and library entry. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes the app data stored on the device. Copies exported to a folder or shared with another app remain in those locations until you remove them there. Temporary export files older than 24 hours are cleaned up during a later app launch. Android may also clear cached files when it needs storage.

Security and user control

Local document copies are stored in Android app-private storage. Export and sharing use Android system mechanisms and temporary file access grants. You can control the files you select, revoke provider access through Android settings, delete documents, clear storage or uninstall the app. The app does not create user accounts, so there is no account deletion process.

Children, future versions and changes

PDF Easy is a general document utility, not a service directed at children or minors. We do not knowingly provide children’s document contents or precise location to advertising services. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact us. Future changes to data practices will be reflected in this policy and the relevant store disclosures.

Contact

For privacy questions, support or a support-message deletion request, contact kedgeyuan@gmail.com.

PDF Easy 隐私政策

开发者：kedge
应用包名：com.kedge.pdfeasy
生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日
联系邮箱：kedgeyuan@gmail.com

本政策涵盖免费且包含 InMobi 横幅广告及 InMobi CMP 隐私选择服务的 1.1.0 版。本版无需账号、无应用内购买。此前的 1.0.0 版没有广告或同意管理 SDK，不进行下述与广告相关的数据传输。

文档和本机处理

阅读、图片转换、PDF 图片导出、DOCX 转换、压缩、合并、批注、水印和手写签名均在设备上完成。应用不向开发者或广告服务传输文档内容、选中的图片、批注、签名、文件名或路径。文档功能仍可离线使用；广告和同意管理服务另行联网，具体如下。

导入的 PDF 会复制到应用私有存储中。文件名称、原文件路径、收藏、标签及颜色、页面书签、阅读位置和设置保存在本机。编辑草稿可能保存在缓存中，用于恢复被中断的编辑会话。签名嵌入到你创建的文档中，不作为可复用签名档案保存。本版不提供云存储或云端备份。

文件选择和分享

打开文件页时，PDF Easy 可申请文件访问权限，以查找手机共享存储中的 PDF。Android 11 及以上使用“所有文件访问”，旧版本使用存储读取权限。扫描只在本机进行，不上传文档内容、名称或路径；不会批量复制所有文件，仅在打开或管理某份文档时创建私有副本。其他应用的私有目录仍不可访问。你可以拒绝或撤销权限，并继续通过 Android 系统选择器单独导入文件。照片也通过系统选择器选择，不申请整个相册的访问权限。云存储等提供方适用各自政策。

只有你主动选择分享时，Android 才向接收应用授予临时文件访问权限；接收应用及你选择的目的地适用各自的隐私政策。是否向外部保存副本由你决定。

PDF 页面导出的 PNG 或 JPG 图片保存在 Pictures/PDF Easy，需由你自行删除。Android 9 及以下仅在导出图片到相册时申请存储写入权限；Android 10 及以上保存这些图片无需申请相册读取或“所有文件访问”权限。

支持邮件

如果你通过邮件应用联系支持，我们会收到你主动发送的邮箱地址、邮件内容及附件，用于回复请求和处理问题。仅在处理这些事项所需的时间内保留支持邮件，法律要求保留的情况除外。你可通过 kedgeyuan@gmail.com 请求删除支持邮件。邮件服务商按照其政策处理邮件传输。

InMobi 广告和隐私选择

1.1.0 版直接接入 InMobi Ads 和 InMobi CMP，不接入 AdMob、Google Mobile Ads、AppLovin MAX 或独立统计分析 SDK。适用隐私要求确认后，文件页可能展示横幅广告；阅读、编辑及转换页不放置横幅。阅读、转换和签名不以观看广告为条件。

InMobi 及其广告合作方可能处理广告标识符、应用集标识符、IP 地址及据此推断的大致位置、设备与系统信息、应用信息、广告展示和互动、性能及诊断事件和同意记录，用于允许范围内的广告展示及个性化、效果衡量、受众统计、广告服务改进和反欺诈。即使你拒绝广告，同意管理服务也可能处理 IP、地区、应用信息及同意记录，以确定适用的隐私提示并记住选择。

PDF Easy 不申请 Android 精确或大致定位权限，不向 SDK 提供 GPS 坐标、通讯录或文档信息。InMobi 的依赖可能包含 Google Play services 的广告标识符或应用集标识符库；这不属于 Google Mobile Ads 接入。

InMobi CMP 管理 GDPR/TCF 及适用的美国隐私选择。在隐私要求尚未确定或缺少必要同意时，不请求广告。拒绝必要同意或选择退出后，会停止后续广告请求并移除横幅。你可在“设置 > 广告与隐私”再次打开选择界面。隐私服务不可用时，文档功能仍可无广告使用。Android 也提供广告 ID 删除或重置功能。隐私选择与本机文档数据分开保存。

InMobi 及其合作方按各自保留规则处理广告数据，数据可能跨境处理。接收方、用途、保留期限和隐私权利申请方式见 InMobi 隐私政策及 CMP 中的供应商披露。可联系开发者寻求协助，或通过 InMobi 的隐私渠道提出申请。清除应用数据会删除本机文档及同意记录，不会自动删除广告提供方已经处理的数据。

保留和删除

在资料库中删除文档，会移除应用内副本和记录。清除应用存储或卸载，会删除设备上的应用数据。导出到文件夹或分享给其他应用的副本需在对应位置自行删除。临时导出文件超过 24 小时后，会在之后启动应用时清理。Android 也可能在存储不足时清理缓存。

安全与控制

本机副本保存在 Android 应用私有存储中；导出和分享使用系统机制及临时文件访问授权。你可选择文件、在 Android 设置中撤销文件提供方的访问、删除文档、清除存储或卸载应用。本版没有用户账号，因此不存在账号注销流程。

儿童、后续版本及政策更新

PDF Easy 是通用文档工具，不面向儿童或未成年人。我们不会明知而向广告服务提供儿童的文档内容或精确位置。如果你认为儿童向我们提供了个人数据，请联系开发者。后续数据处理方式变化时，会更新本政策及商店披露。
