PDF Easy Privacy Policy

Developer: kedge
App: PDF Easy: Read, Convert & Sign
Package: com.kedge.pdfeasy
Effective date: 14 October 2026
Contact: kedgeyuan@gmail.com

This policy describes the free, ad-free first release of PDF Easy. It has no accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, in-app purchases or automatic data uploads.

Documents and local processing

Reading, image conversion, PDF image export, DOCX conversion, compression, merging, annotations, watermarks and handwritten signing take place on your device. PDF Easy does not transmit your document contents, selected images, notes or signatures to the developer or a server. It does not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, usage analytics or diagnostic reports.

Imported PDFs are copied into private app storage. Document names, original file paths, favorites, tags and tag colors, page bookmarks, reading positions and preferences are stored locally. An editing draft may be kept in the app cache to restore an interrupted session. A signature is embedded into a document you create and is not saved as a reusable signature profile. The app does not offer cloud storage or cloud backup.

File selection and sharing

When you open Files, PDF Easy can request file access to discover PDF documents across shared storage. Android 11 and later use All files access; older Android versions use storage read permission. The app scans locally without uploading document contents, names or paths. It lists accessible PDFs without copying every file, and creates a private copy when you open or manage one. Other apps’ private folders remain inaccessible. You may decline or revoke this permission and continue choosing individual documents through the Android system picker. Photos also use the system picker, without broad photo-library permission. A selected cloud provider operates under its own policy.

Sharing occurs only when you choose to share a file. Android grants temporary access to the receiving app. That app and any destination you select process the shared content under their own policies. You decide whether to save copies outside the app.

Support email

If you choose to contact support through your email app, we receive the email address, message and attachments you send. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve the reported issue. Support messages are retained only as needed for that purpose, unless retention is required by law. You may request deletion by contacting kedgeyuan@gmail.com. Your email provider handles delivery under its own policy.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a document from the library deletes its app-private copy and library entry. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes the app data stored on the device. Copies exported to a folder or shared with another app remain in those locations until you remove them there. Temporary export files older than 24 hours are cleaned up during a later app launch. Android may also clear cached files when it needs storage.

Security and user control

Local document copies are stored in Android app-private storage. Export and sharing use Android system mechanisms and temporary file access grants. You can control the files you select, revoke provider access through Android settings, delete documents, clear storage or uninstall the app. The app does not create user accounts, so there is no account deletion process.

Children, future versions and changes

PDF Easy is a general document utility, not a service designed specifically for children. This release has no advertising or tracking. If future releases introduce advertising, subscriptions or different data practices, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, support or a support-message deletion request, contact kedgeyuan@gmail.com.

PDF Easy 隐私政策

开发者：kedge
应用包名：com.kedge.pdfeasy
生效日期：2026 年 10 月 14 日
联系邮箱：kedgeyuan@gmail.com

本政策适用于 PDF Easy 免费、无广告的首版。本版不提供账号、广告、跟踪分析、应用内购买或自动数据上传。

文档和本机处理

阅读、图片转换、PDF 图片导出、DOCX 转换、压缩、合并、批注、水印和手写签名均在设备上完成。应用不向开发者或服务器传输文档、选中的图片、批注和签名；不收集设备或广告标识符、位置、使用分析或诊断报告。

导入的 PDF 会复制到应用私有存储中。文件名称、原文件路径、收藏、标签及颜色、页面书签、阅读位置和设置保存在本机。编辑草稿可能保存在缓存中，用于恢复被中断的编辑会话。签名嵌入到你创建的文档中，不作为可复用签名档案保存。本版不提供云存储或云端备份。

文件选择和分享

打开文件页时，PDF Easy 可申请文件访问权限，以查找手机共享存储中的 PDF。Android 11 及以上使用“所有文件访问”，旧版本使用存储读取权限。扫描只在本机进行，不上传文档内容、名称或路径；不会批量复制所有文件，仅在打开或管理某份文档时创建私有副本。其他应用的私有目录仍不可访问。你可以拒绝或撤销权限，并继续通过 Android 系统选择器单独导入文件。照片也通过系统选择器选择，不申请整个相册的访问权限。云存储等提供方适用各自政策。

只有你主动选择分享时，Android 才向接收应用授予临时文件访问权限；接收应用及你选择的目的地适用各自的隐私政策。是否向外部保存副本由你决定。

支持邮件

如果你通过邮件应用联系支持，我们会收到你主动发送的邮箱地址、邮件内容及附件，用于回复请求和处理问题。仅在处理这些事项所需的时间内保留支持邮件，法律要求保留的情况除外。你可通过 kedgeyuan@gmail.com 请求删除支持邮件。邮件服务商按照其政策处理邮件传输。

保留和删除

在资料库中删除文档，会移除应用内副本和记录。清除应用存储或卸载，会删除设备上的应用数据。导出到文件夹或分享给其他应用的副本需在对应位置自行删除。临时导出文件超过 24 小时后，会在之后启动应用时清理。Android 也可能在存储不足时清理缓存。

安全与控制

本机副本保存在 Android 应用私有存储中；导出和分享使用系统机制及临时文件访问授权。你可选择文件、在 Android 设置中撤销文件提供方的访问、删除文档、清除存储或卸载应用。本版没有用户账号，因此不存在账号注销流程。

儿童、后续版本及政策更新

PDF Easy 是通用文档工具，并非专门为儿童设计的服务。本版不展示广告或进行跟踪。如果后续版本引入广告、订阅或其他数据处理方式，我们会在发布这些变化前更新本政策和商店披露。

联系

隐私问题、支持或支持邮件删除请求，请联系 kedgeyuan@gmail.com。
