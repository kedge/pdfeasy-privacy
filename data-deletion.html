PDF Easy has no user accounts and does not upload your documents, photos, signatures, file names or paths to the developer or advertising services.

How to delete data or make a request

  1. On-device documents and settings: delete a document in the app to remove its private copy and library entry. Clear PDF Easy storage in Android settings, or uninstall it, to remove local documents, tags, bookmarks, drafts, preferences and consent records. Exported PDF/PNG/JPG files remain in their chosen folders or gallery until you delete those copies. Temporary exports older than 24 hours are cleaned on a later app launch.
  2. Support emails: email kedgeyuan@gmail.com with the subject “PDF Easy data deletion”, and identify the support messages or attachments you want deleted. Do not attach documents, signatures, passwords or identity documents. We may ask for the minimum information needed to verify the request. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request or meet a legal retention obligation; we do not promise an automatic 90-day deletion period.
  3. Advertising and consent data: version 1.1.0 uses InMobi Ads and InMobi CMP. Reopen Settings → Advertising privacy to change consent or opt out; Android also provides advertising-ID controls. These actions stop future ad requests where applicable and remove local choices when app storage is cleared; they do not automatically erase records held by InMobi or its partners. Use the privacy-rights request channels described in the InMobi privacy policy, or contact us for help directing the request. InMobi and its partners handle requests, verification and any legally required or fraud-prevention retention under their policies and applicable law. We do not claim that all partner records are deleted immediately or within a fixed period.

There is no developer-hosted document archive or cloud account to delete. Other apps receiving a file through a share action handle that copy under their own policies.
